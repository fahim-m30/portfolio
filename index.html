Presented (Awaiting Publication) 2026 IEEE SPICSCON • BAUET

A Hybrid Synthetic Oversampling and Federated Learning Framework for Class-Imbalanced Intrusion Detection in Heterogeneous Networks

Authors: Snaholata Mondal, Md. Julker Nain Rakib, Fahim Muntasir (IUBAT)

Network intrusion datasets are overwhelmingly biased toward benign traffic, leading conventional models to fail on rare zero-day cyber-attacks. This research introduces a hybrid framework combining synthetic oversampling (SMOTE, SMOTE-Tomek, GAN-based SMOTE) with 6 Federated Learning algorithms (FedAvg, FedProx, FedDyn, SCAFFOLD, DP-Fed, FedOpt) and SHAP/LIME explainability across CICIDS2017 & CIC-UNSW-NB15 networks.

Federated Learning (6 Alg.) GAN-based SMOTE Intrusion Detection (IDS) SHAP & LIME (XAI) Cybersecurity

Deep Dive
Submitted Manuscript Medical Imaging Journal

An Explainable and Class-Imbalance-Aware Deep Learning Framework for Multi-Class Alzheimer’s Disease Severity Classification from Brain MRI

Accurately diagnosing early stages of Alzheimer’s disease from structural brain MRI scans is vital for patient prognosis. This study formulates an end-to-end deep learning framework equipped with class-imbalance mitigation and Grad-CAM visual interpretability, allowing clinicians to inspect and validate the specific neuro-anatomical regions guiding classification decisions.

Deep Learning Alzheimer’s Detection Explainable AI (Grad-CAM) Brain MRI PyTorch / CNNs
